03 / THE STORY

From performance
to purpose.

I began on stage — performing, creating and learning how attention works in real time. That journey led to television, brand collaborations and more than 200 live performances.

But the deeper lesson was not performance. It was understanding people: what makes them listen, what makes them trust and what makes an idea stay with them.

Today, I bring that instinct into content strategy, brand storytelling and founder-led communication across India, Saudi Arabia and Ireland.
